1. TOM TAT
----------
He thong gom 3 luong chinh:

A. Reference pipeline:
   provinces -> candidate places -> validate -> classify -> places.csv

B. Social ingestion:
   places.csv -> Google Maps ratings/reviews
              -> YouTube videos -> YouTube comments

C. Lakehouse pipeline:
   Bronze/CSV -> dbt -> DuckDB -> Iceberg -> Lakekeeper -> MinIO warehouse

Pipeline co the chay binh thuong va dung giua chung. Du lieu da ghi thanh cong
khong bi xoa. Khi chay lai, checkpoint loc bo item da SUCCESS.

Lich: schedule=None, chi chay khi trigger thu cong.

    -> quality_gate_provinces
    -> crawl_candidate_places
    -> quality_gate_candidates
    -> validate_places_spatially
    -> quality_gate_validated_places

6. DUNG GIUA CHUNG VA RESUME
-----------------------------
Co the dung pipeline khi dang chay.

Cach nen dung:
  - Vao Airflow UI.
  - Mo task dang chay.
  - Dung task/run theo chuc nang Stop/Mark Failed cua Airflow.

Khong nen dung docker compose stop airflow-scheduler neu chi muon dung mot task,
vi lenh nay dung ca scheduler va co the anh huong cac task khac.

Khi nhan SIGTERM, crawler co gang:
  - Ghi metrics hien tai.
  - In snapshot vao Airflow log.
  - Gui Discord [STOPPED].
  - Thoat voi ma 143.

Item da SUCCESS:
  - Duoc ghi checkpoint.
  - Lan chay sau bi bo qua khi TLCN_REFRESH_SUCCESS=false.

Item dang crawl do:
  - Co the chua co checkpoint SUCCESS.
  - Lan sau se crawl lai tu dau cua item do.
  - Khong resume giua chung ben trong mot place/video.

Item FAILED:
  - Duoc ghi FAILED.
  - Lan sau co the duoc thu lai.

Khong xoa data/bronze/_state neu muon resume.

7. ALERT DISCORD VA PROGRESS
----------------------------
Alert cap task/crawler:
  - [PROGRESS]: gui theo batch, mac dinh moi 10 item.
  - [STOPPED]: gui ngay khi task bi dung, kem snapshot.

Airflow callback:
  - [SUCCESS]: gui khi ca DAG thanh cong.
  - [FAILED]: gui khi task/DAG that bai.

Airflow log van co progress tung item, vi du:
  progress: 20/1000
  success: 19 | failed: 1
  remaining: 980

Dieu chinh tan suat Discord trong .env:
  DISCORD_PROGRESS_EVERY=10

Dat 20 neu muon chi gui moi 20 item. Khong nen gui sau moi item khi co hang
nghin places vi se lam spam kenh Discord.

  YOUTUBE_MAX_PLACES=0

  YOUTUBE_MAX_COMMENTS=5

van phu thuoc max video/comment moi item.

  TLCN_REFRESH_SUCCESS=false

Khong dat true trong van hanh thuong. true se crawl lai ca item da thanh cong.

Tier:
  TLCN_CRAWL_TIERS=tier_1,tier_2,tier_3

Sau khi sua .env, nap lai cho Airflow:
  docker compose up -d --force-recreate airflow-scheduler airflow-webserver

9. LUU TRU BRONZE
------------------
Bronze duoc ghi song song vao local va MinIO.

Local:
  data/bronze/<dataset>/crawl_date=YYYY-MM-DD/part-<run_id>.jsonl

MinIO:
  bucket bronze/<dataset>/crawl_date=YYYY-MM-DD/part-<run_id>.jsonl

Dataset mau:
  bronze/google_maps/ratings
  bronze/google_maps/reviews
  bronze/google_maps/errors
  bronze/youtube/videos
  bronze/youtube/comments
  bronze/youtube/crawl_log
  bronze/youtube/comments_crawl_log
  bronze/osm/candidate_places_raw
  bronze/osm/candidate_places_validated
  bronze/places
  bronze/reference/provinces
  bronze/reference/place_categories
  bronze/reference/taxonomy

Bucket lakehouse la bucket chung, phan tach bang prefix:
  lakehouse/bronze/ raw Bronze
  lakehouse/raw/    raw input cho cac layer lakehouse
  lakehouse/iceberg/ du lieu Iceberg neu duoc su dung

Record Bronze co metadata:
  run_id
  source
  source_id
  source_url
  ingested_at_utc
  pipeline_version
  crawler_version
  attempt
  record_status

Record khong dat quality gate duoc ghi vao:
  data/bronze/quarantine/<dataset>/...

10. STATE, METRICS VA BACKFILL MARKER
-------------------------------------
Checkpoint:
  data/bronze/_state/*.jsonl
  Luu SUCCESS/FAILED theo place_id hoac video_id.

Metrics:
  data/bronze/_metrics/*.json
  Luu tong so item, success, failed, skipped, duration va success_rate.

Backfill marker:
  data/bronze/_backfill/*.json
  Danh dau CSV lich su da backfill de tranh nap lap.

Backfill historical thuoc social DAG, khong thuoc reference DAG.

Reference DAG khong tu dong nap toan bo data/historical.
